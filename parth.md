**Private · In development**

Nexcore AI is an AI assistant project exploring a modular, context-aware
experience across voice and text interaction.

The goal is to develop an assistant that can understand user
instructions, respond conversationally, and support practical digital
workflows. The project is an ongoing learning and engineering effort.

**Current areas of exploration** - AI-powered conversational
